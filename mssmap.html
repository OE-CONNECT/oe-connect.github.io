Manuscript map

Use the map and filters to find manuscripts and fragments containing Old English in continental European collections.

The manuscripts and fragments listed below are provided with very short descriptions of the Old English contents, as well as links that redirect to specific folios and/or full digital facsimiles (if available).

The blue links at the bottom of the manuscript descriptions refer to:

  • Ker: Ker, N. (1957). Catalogue of Manuscripts containing Anglo-Saxon (Oxford: Clarendon Press) + addenda, links redirecting to Linked Data URIs in Searobend: Linked Metadata for English-Language Texts, 1000-1300.
  • Electronic Gneuss-Lapidge: Gneuss, H., & Lapidge, M. (2014). Anglo-Saxon Manuscripts: A Bibliographical Handlist of Manuscripts and Manuscript Fragments Written or Owned in England up to 1100 (Toronto: University of Toronto Press), as digitized by A Consolidated Library of Anglo-Saxon Poetry [CLASP]
  • ASMMF: Manuscript descriptions in Anglo-Saxon Manuscripts in Microfiche Facsimile, ed. A. N. Doane & Matthew T. Hussey
  • Studer-Joho: Studer-Joho, D. (2017). A Catalogue of Manuscripts Known to Contain Old English Dry-Point Glosses (Tübingen: Narr Francke Attempto)
  • OEiS: Seiler, A., & Studer-Joho, N. (Eds.). (2025). Old English in Switzerland: Manuscripts, Texts and Libraries (Basel: Schwabe Verlag)
