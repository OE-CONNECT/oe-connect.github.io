OE-CONNECT

Old English Continental Network: Collaboration, Research and Teaching

OE-CONNECT aims to create a sustainable interdisciplinary network of researchers and teachers working on the cultural heritage of early medieval England (c. 500–1100 CE) at continental European institutions. This collaborative project focuses on Old English language and literature, manuscripts and the post-medieval reception of this heritage. Core activities include a hybrid lecture series, a symposium and setting up an online platform with a repository of teaching materials, an overview of existing projects/events and an interactive map of Old English manuscripts in European libraries and archives. As such, OE-CONNECT hopes to enhance research excellence and stimulate teaching innovation.
